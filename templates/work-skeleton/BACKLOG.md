# Backlog

Leads and known issues found while reading, profiling, or experimenting. One line each, with the evidence, so a proposal starts from facts.

- Add a lead when you notice it, even mid-experiment. Measured numbers beat guesses; write "not measured" when there are none.
- When a lead becomes an experiment, point to it; when it is ruled out, point to the decision. Delete an entry once its experiment or decision fully records it.

Entry format: `- [open | exp-NNN | ruled out D-NNN] <lead>: <evidence, with numbers and where they came from>`
